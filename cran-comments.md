# CRAN submission comments: inflateR 0.2.1

## Reason for this submission

Data patch. The World Bank has not published a 2025 US CPI (the US
statistical agency missed one month of collection in 2025), so US CPI
conversions stopped at 2024 while every other currency reached 2025. The
2025 value is now chained from the official US annual average. Details
in NEWS.md.
